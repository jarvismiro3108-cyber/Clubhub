Privacy Policy

Club Hub is a free, independent football fan site and app. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no phone numbers.
  • No advertising, no ad trackers and no analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No location data, contacts, photos or any other personal data from your device.

What stays on your device

The line-ups, formations and settings you choose are saved in your own browser or app storage on your device (local storage) so they are still there next time. They are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or by deleting the app.

What is sent when you press “Play the match”

To write the match analysis, the app sends the match details to our server and then to Google's Gemini AI service: the two team names, the venue, the formation, the chosen players' names and positions (public information about professional footballers), and the numbers from our prediction model. Nothing about you is included.

  • Our server runs on Cloudflare. Like every website, it receives your IP address when you connect. We use it only for a few seconds to limit how often one device can ask the AI (to stop abuse) and to work out your country (see below). We do not store it or build any profile.
  • Google Gemini receives the match details above. Club Hub uses Google's free Gemini service, under which Google may keep the requests and answers and use them to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. That is one more reason we never send personal information. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.
  • Google Search may be used by Gemini to check current team form. When that happens, the page shows Google's search suggestions and links; opening them takes you to Google or the source site, which have their own privacy policies.

Country check

Google's free Gemini service may not be offered to people in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom. If you are there, the AI analysis is switched off and you get the built-in match simulation instead. The country comes from Cloudflare's network and is not stored.

Children

Club Hub is a general sports app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions or requests: contact us here.

Data sources

  • Season tables up to 2021–22 are rebuilt by us from match results in the open worldfootballR_data collection (originally from FBref). Later seasons are taken from public league tables.
  • Club facts (founding dates, stadiums, honours, records) are summarised in our own words from public sources such as Wikipedia. Facts themselves are not owned by anyone; no Wikipedia text is copied.
  • 2026/27 squads are compiled from public squad lists (club websites, VAVEL, Wikipedia and others).
  • Current league tables, results, goalscorers and player stats (goals, assists, saves) are loaded live from ESPN's public sports data and refreshed every few minutes. They can occasionally be delayed or incomplete.
  • Champions League and Europa League tables and knockout ties also come from ESPN. Projected brackets, the Bracket Simulator and Fantasy Draft gameweeks are simulations made by Club Hub and are not real results.
  • Match analysis text is generated by Google Gemini, sometimes using Google Search.
